Confidence · 0 to 100

Is it real and material?

score = 100 × (0.30·Say + 0.35·Do + 0.35·Pay) × skeptic penalty

Penalties are rules, not vibes: one account holds most of the dollars, only one source, or complainers don’t use the feature.

A design fix I made to my own first draft: I originally mapped patterns to score bands, mirage at 40 to 60 and silent churn at 60 to 80. That mixes two questions. A silent churn risk can be 90 percent certain. So confidence and pattern are separate outputs. Weights are a starting point; the closed loop on outcomes is how they get learned.
