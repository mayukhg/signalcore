How I'd run prioritisation in your org

A 90-day pilot on your own data

Days 1–30

Baseline

Export 6 months of calls, tickets, product analytics, CRM and Jira. Score today's prioritisation list by CD3. Find the two weakest commitments and the costliest blocker.

Days 31–60

Ritual

Run weekly triage from the matrix. Every new PRD cites Say, Do and Pay. First grounded PBR to the SLT.

Days 61–90

Prove it

Act on one silent-churn finding with CS. Measure adoption and renewal on the accounts touched.

Success metric: share of committed roadmap backed by evidence ≥ 70, from [__]% today to [__]%.
