The insight

Believe a theme only when three independent sources agree

Say

What they say

Gong and Zoom calls, Zendesk and Jira tickets, idea portal, advisory-board notes

Pain clusters, urgency, buyer vs admin, workarounds

Do

What they do

Pendo or Mixpanel events, funnels, abandonment, time on task, usage trends

Real friction, and whether complainers even use the feature

Pay

What they pay

Salesforce or HubSpot: ARR, renewal dates, pipeline, win/loss notes, ICP fit

Revenue at stake, and whether it is spread or concentrated

Each pillar alone is easy to game or misread. Agreement across all three is hard to fake.

SignalCore · Interview pitch

The core idea is triangulation. Qualitative data is loud but biased toward whoever talks most. Telemetry is honest but has no “why”. Revenue tells you what matters but is easily dominated by one deal. SignalCore cross-examines each theme across all three before it reaches the roadmap.
